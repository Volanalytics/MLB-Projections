17 · Methodology & Audit Rules

Projection is the quantitative prior; market comparison identifies discrepancies; intelligence validates or invalidates assumptions. Lifecycle: DISCOVERED → AUDIT → CONDITIONAL → SUPPORTED → ACTIONABLE → FROZEN, with backward moves allowed. Moneyline EV may be calculated from model win probability and verified price. Totals must not be assigned invented win probabilities; use calibrated simulation probability if available, otherwise report model-vs-market run gap plus intelligence confidence. Preserve null/unresolved states and evidence-layer separation.
