DEFINITIONS

n
Graded games for that model and window.
MAE
Mean absolute error between the projected total and the actual runs scored. Lower is a tighter point estimate.
bias
Mean signed error, projection minus actual. Positive means the variant projects high, negative means it projects low.
Totals W-L-P
Wins, losses and pushes from backing whichever side the projection implies against the posted total.
Totals ROI
Profit divided by the number of settled bets, at the price actually offered. Pushes are excluded from the divisor.
ML bets
Moneyline bets recorded, meaning games where the model's win probability exceeded the book's no-vig probability by at least the edge threshold.
ML ROI
Profit per moneyline bet placed, at the offered price.
no-vig
The book's two prices converted to probabilities and rescaled to sum to 1, removing the margin built into the quote.
edge bucket
How far the projected total sat from the posted line, in runs, at the time of the bet.

Moneyline edge threshold: 3 percentage points. Totals are graded against BetOnline only; Kalshi lists totals as per-threshold contracts whose strike often differs from the posted line.
