EV% for the same bet under every window. Season is the full-season rates. L30d, L14d, L7d are calendar windows — trailing days. L15g, L7g, L3g are game-count windows — each player's last N games played, read only from today's side of the venue. The d and g suffixes matter: L7d is seven days, L7g is seven games. The small grey number is that window's projection — the projected total on a total, the model's win probability on a moneyline — and it matches the Games and Form pages. Sorted by spread, widest disagreement first.

Count agreement by family, not by column. Every window shares the same lineups, park factor, bullpen, umpire and simulator, and windows within one model share their rate construction too — four calendar windows agreeing is close to one opinion stated four times, not four confirmations. The short windows are also regressed hard toward a season anchor, so they agree with the season column by construction. The sub-counts (c for calendar, g for games) are the useful read: a row where one family is unanimous one way and the other unanimous the opposite is marked opposed, and that is the only disagreement here backed by two genuinely different readings of form.

The game-count windows are not backtested. The calibration constants and the edge figures on Tracking were both measured on season rates and do not carry over.

Each model against completed games it projected beforehand. MAE is here because it is conventional, but it is a poor way to separate these — almost all of it is irreducible game noise, and a model that improved meaningfully would barely move it. Bias and the over/under call rate are more sensitive; closing-line value is better still.

Model output, not advice. Held-out error is about 3.7 runs a game and the model does not yet beat a league-average constant — treat every number as a wide estimate.
