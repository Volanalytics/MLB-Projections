The of stake column is the error as a share of the cheaper side's price, which is what a mispricing actually costs you: 1.9 points on a 10% contract is 19% of the stake, the same 1.9 points at 50% is under 4%. The ± column bootstraps over GAMES, not strikes, because twenty strikes from one game are one game's worth of evidence.
