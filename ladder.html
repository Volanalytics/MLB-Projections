Binned by what the model CLAIMED, not by the line — the error depends on the claim. A -110 bet needs 52.4% to break even, so a bin off by more than 2.4 points cannot support a wager at even money — but that yardstick only applies near 50%. The of stake column is the error as a share of the cheaper side's price, which is what a mispricing actually costs you: 1.9 points on a 10% contract is 19% of the stake, the same 1.9 points at 50% is under 4%. A strike is called unusable only when it clears both that bar and 1.5 standard errors. The ± column bootstraps over GAMES, not strikes, because twenty strikes from one game are one game's worth of evidence.
